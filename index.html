About IDR

An industry-led institute, not a classroom

We sit between universities and employers — translating academic frameworks into deployable, audit-ready risk talent.

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and technology risk roles in regulated environments.

We collaborate with UK universities and industry partners to combine academic insight with real-world digital risk projects — so every graduate has handled live casework before their first day on the job.

Our practitioners go on to roles across financial services, critical infrastructure and regulated technology — built to operate from day one, not ramp up over a year.

Our Model

Three functions, one pipeline

Training, research and advisory work feed each other — case material from real engagements goes straight back into the curriculum.

Academy

Intensive training bootcamps for digital risk professionals, built around real regulatory casework and simulated incidents.

Innovation

Developing AI governance frameworks and applied risk models alongside academic and enterprise research partners.

Advisory

Helping organisations implement frameworks like NIST CSF and ISO 27001 — from gap assessment through to certification.
